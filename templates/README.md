# Bring your own template 🖼️

Drop your corporate `.pptx` or `.potx` files in this folder (or upload them straight in the app sidebar).

What SlideForge does with your template:

1. **Keeps** all slide masters, layouts, background art, logos, colours and fonts.
2. **Clears** any existing slides (many people upload a finished deck as their "template" — that's fine).
3. **Extracts** the OOXML colour scheme (`accent1…6`, `dk1/lt1`) and font scheme, so even
   fully custom-drawn slides (KPI cards, process chevrons, comparison cards) match your brand.
4. **Matches** each generated slide to your best-fitting layout — title slides to *Title Slide*,
   sections to *Section Header*, bullets to *Title and Content*, and drawn layouts to your
   blank/title-only canvas.

Tips for best results:

- Templates exported from PowerPoint via *File → Save As → PowerPoint Template (.potx)* work great.
- 16:9 templates look best; other sizes are respected too.
- If a template can't be parsed, SlideForge automatically falls back to a built-in theme —
  generation never fails because of a template.
